# Code of Conduct

## Short version

Be kind. Be specific. Assume good faith. Disagree on substance, not people.

## Long version

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Expected behaviour:

- Be welcoming to new contributors, especially those new to OSS.
- Critique code, not people. "This function has a bug" not "you wrote a buggy function".
- Acknowledge that "no" is a complete sentence — maintainers may decline features without owing you a treatise.
- Treat users sharing PII or job-search struggles in issues with discretion.

Unacceptable:

- Personal attacks, slurs, harassment.
- Posting other people's resumes, employers, or salary info without permission.
- Doxxing the project's contributors.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory at https://github.com/drajb/Job-Application-Automation/security/advisories/new with subject "Code of Conduct".

## Enforcement

Maintainers will investigate and may take action including a warning, a temporary block from project spaces, or a permanent ban. We will not name parties publicly unless they're a repeat offender or the incident is already public.
